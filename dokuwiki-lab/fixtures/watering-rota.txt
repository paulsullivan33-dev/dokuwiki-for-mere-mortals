====== The Watering Rota ======

Water early, before the heat of the day. One full can per bed, two for tomatoes, three in a heat wave.

  * Monday — Amara
  * Wednesday — Ben
  * Friday — Priya
  * Sunday — whoever is passing

If rain is forecast, skip the day and note it in the
[[gardening:season-notes|season notes]].
